Medea Creek

Arts Collective

An informal group of artists and makers in Oak Park, California. We started in 2026 and we’re looking for our first members.

Potters, painters, printmakers, woodworkers, jewelers, fiber artists — anyone making things in and around Oak Park. Membership is open to anyone in the area who makes work. There’s no jury and no portfolio review, because we’re a neighborhood group, not a gallery.

We’re named for the creek that runs through town, not for the middle school of the same name. We have no connection to the school or the school district.

Meander

Our first project is an app. Meander is a free, private archive for the art your family makes: your children’s work and your own. Photograph a piece, say whose it is, and Meander keeps it by artist and year. No ads, no tracking. It is in testing now, and we are looking for families to try it.

About Meander and the beta

What we’re planning

  • Meander, the family art archive, in testing now
  • A public directory of local artists, so people can find and commission work nearby
  • A community craft fair, aiming for this winter
  • Open studio days, small and low-stakes
  • A scholarship for a graduating Oak Park student in the visual arts

Meander is the first of these. The rest is early, and nothing is scheduled yet.

Who we are

Started by Ryan and Kate, both in Oak Park. Ryan builds software. Kate makes ceramics.
