Medea Creek

Arts Collective

Medea Creek runs south through Oak Park and Agoura Hills, ignoring the county line, the school district boundary, and fifty years of habit.

We're a new, informal group of artists and makers doing the same. Potters, painters, printmakers, woodworkers, jewellers, fibre artists — anyone making things in this corner of the valley.

No jury. No portfolio review. If you make things here, you're in.

What we're building

  • A public directory of local artists, so people can find and commission work nearby
  • A community craft fair, planned for autumn
  • Open studio days, small and low-stakes
  • A scholarship for a graduating local student in the visual arts

Founding members wanted

We're looking for the first dozen or so people. If that's you — or if you just want to hear when something's happening — say hello.
