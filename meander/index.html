A private archive for the art your family makes: your children’s work and your own, sorted for you.

Photograph a drawing, a painting or a sculpture, and say whose work it is. Meander keeps each piece by artist and year, with its medium and subject. Up to four photos show one piece from every side, in the order you choose.

Find a piece again from Works, or look through Albums: each artist’s pieces by year, and smart albums that group pieces by their details. Make a collection and send it as a link, or make a portfolio page for one artist. Invite family members to the archive, and choose what each one can see.

Our promise

  • Free. No price, no in-app purchases.
  • Private. Only you and the people you invite see your archive. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Yours. Download a copy of everything at any time, or delete the archive in Settings.
  • Kept. Pieces you add during the test move to the real Meander when it opens.

Testing now

Meander is an early test build from the Medea Creek Arts Collective. We are looking for a small group of families to try it, on iPhone and on the web, and tell us what is confusing or broken.

iPhone

TestFlight

Meander installs through Apple’s TestFlight app. Four taps, on your iPhone:

  1. Tap Join the iPhone test.
  2. If asked, get TestFlight from the App Store, then tap the link again.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Meander from your home screen.

Web, and Android phones

A sign-up code

Meander runs in any browser. During the test, starting an archive on the web needs a code. Ask us for one, and sign in with Google or a link sent to your email.

Ask for a web code

If you already started on your iPhone, the web needs no code: sign in the same way at dev.medeacreekarts.org. Someone invited to a family member’s archive needs no code either.

What to test

  1. Sign in with Apple, Google, or a link sent to your email.
  2. Add a few pieces of art from your photos, and say whose work each piece is.
  3. Look at Works and Albums, and fix a detail on one piece.
  4. Make a collection and share its link with someone.
  5. Invite a family member from the Family tab.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the TestFlight build, or write to hello@medeacreekarts.org. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

Questions

Who can see my archive?

Only you and the people you invite on the Family tab, each seeing what you allow. A collection you share as a link or a portfolio page is seen by whoever has the link.

What happens to my pieces when the test ends?

They move with you. When Meander opens for everyone, your archive, your photos and your collections carry over to the real service. You sign in the same way. Shared links from the test get a new address, and the old one says where it went.

Does Meander work on Android?

In the browser, yes. An Android app comes later.

Where are the details?

The test build’s Support page, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Everything Meander keeps is stored in the United States on Google Cloud, in the Collective’s own projects.
