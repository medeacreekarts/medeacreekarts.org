# Instagram posts

Five 1080 × 1350 images made from the app screenshots (`shots.py`, next to the raw screenshots,
outside the repo). Set the bio link to https://medeacreekarts.org/meander before the first post.

## Post 1: Meander, introduced

Image: `01-friends.jpg` alone, or the brand kit's `instagram-post.png` (the mark on Bisque).

> This is Meander, an app from the Medea Creek Arts Collective.
>
> It started with a box. Every family with a child who draws has one: paintings from preschool,
> clay from third grade, a sketchbook from last summer. The box fills, and nobody can find anything
> in it. Meander is a home for that work. Photograph a piece, say whose it is, and it is kept by
> artist and year. Your own work goes in too.
>
> Private, free, no ads. Made in Oak Park. More soon.

## Post 2: the tease and the call for testers

Slides: `01-friends.jpg`, then `02-works.jpg`.

> Meander is an app for the art your family makes. Photograph a piece, say whose it is, and it is

> We are testing it now with a small group of families on iPhone and the web. Want in? Message us
